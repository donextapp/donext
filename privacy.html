Privacy policy

Your homework stays on your phone.

Do Next has no account, no servers, no ads and no tracking. We don’t collect anything about you. This page explains exactly what that means.

Last updated October 27, 2026

The short version

  • Everything you put in Do Next is saved only on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch if you use one.
  • Nex, the assistant, uses Apple’s AI model running on the phone itself. Your questions aren’t sent anywhere.
  • We don’t collect, sell or share any information. There are no analytics and no advertising.
  • If a future feature ever needs your data to leave your phone, you’ll be told first, in plain words, and it will be your choice.

What Do Next keeps, and where

Your tasks, classes, homework times, Memory (things like weekly practice and your own rules), the times Do Next learns from finished work, and any Nex reply you mark with thumbs-down are saved in the app on your iPhone. If you have an Apple Watch, it gets a copy from the phone so you can add and see tasks there. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Deleting the app deletes all of it from your phone.

The AI and your voice

Nex runs on Apple’s on-device model (part of Apple Intelligence), so what you type or ask is handled on your phone. On phones without Apple Intelligence, Nex still works, using rules built into the app. When you talk to Do Next, your voice is turned into text on the phone; voice input only works where the phone can do that itself.

When something leaves your phone

Only in these cases, and never to us unless you send it:

iCloud backup
If iCloud Backup is on, your iPhone backup includes Do Next, like your other apps. Apple encrypts and stores it under Apple’s privacy policy.
Siri
If you use Siri with Do Next (“Ask Do Next”, “Add to Do Next”), Siri handles the request as it does for any app.
Tips
Tips are App Store purchases, handled by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only keeps a count of tips on your phone, to say thanks.
Reports and ideas you send
If Nex gets something wrong, you can tap thumbs-down and choose to email that conversation to the developer. Suggest a feature works the same way. You see the whole email and send it yourself, from your own email app. We use these emails only to fix and improve Do Next. To have one deleted, email us.

Permissions it asks for

  • Notifications, for reminders. They’re scheduled on the phone, not sent from a server.
  • Microphone and speech recognition, only while you’re talking to Do Next, to turn what you say into a task or a question.
  • Siri, if you want to use Do Next with Siri or the Action Button.

You can turn any of these off in the iPhone’s Settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Students and children

Do Next is made for high school students. Because it doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, it doesn’t collect any from children under 13 either.

If this ever changes

If a future feature needs your data to leave your phone, it will be off until you turn it on, it will say in plain words what leaves and why before you do, and this page will be updated before that feature ships.

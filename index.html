Support

What to do next, and when you’re free.

Do Next puts your most pressing homework first, plans tonight around the time you actually have, and keeps reminders out of the way on nights you’re busy. Everything runs on your phone.

Need help?

Email donext.reports@gmail.com and say what happened. If it’s about something Nex said, the quickest way is in the app: tap thumbs-down on that reply and choose to email it, so the conversation comes with it.

Common questions

I’m not getting reminders

Check that notifications are on for Do Next in the iPhone’s Settings → Notifications. Reminders only come inside your evening window (Settings in Do Next), never on a night you’ve marked out, and they wait while you’re locked in on Tonight. Gentle sends fewer than Standard.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Everything works without it, and Nex still understands adding, moving, checking off and questions about your week. On phones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Nex can also handle looser, chattier questions.

Nex got something wrong

Tap thumbs-down on the reply and say what you meant. You can email it to the developer from there. These reports are how Nex gets better every week.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and pick Do Next. The large widget lets you check things off. For tonight’s card on the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen and tap Customize.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. With Do Next on your iPhone, open it on your paired Apple Watch to see what’s next and add a task quietly in class.

I got a new phone. Will my tasks come with me?

Yes, if you move to the new phone from an iCloud backup or straight from the old phone. Do Next has no account, so your tasks live only in your phone and its backup.

New semester, new classes

In Do Next, open Settings and run setup again. Your classes and homework times start fresh, and your tasks stay.

What are tips for?

They’re optional, and nothing in the app is locked behind them. They help pay the $99 a year Apple charges to keep Do Next on the App Store.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Do Next saved is on your phone, so that removes all of it. Nothing is stored anywhere else. See the privacy policy.

Have an idea?

In Do Next, open the ⓘ menu and tap Suggest a feature, or tell Nex “can you add…”. Every idea gets read.
